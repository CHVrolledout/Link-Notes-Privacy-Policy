Link Notes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

Link Notes is an Android app by onesectolive ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything you save stays on your phone. Link Notes has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics, and I never receive your data.

What Link Notes stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage:

  • Links you save and details about them: the page title, the creator's name and a small preview picture.
  • Your notes: what you type or say.
  • Voice notes you record, and the text written out from them so they can be searched.
  • Tags and your settings (accent colour, theme, voice language).

None of this is sent to me or to any server I run. I don't run any servers.

Internet: fetching titles and pictures

When you save a link, Link Notes asks the website the link points to for its title and preview picture, the same way a browser would when you open the link. For example, a YouTube link is looked up on YouTube, an Instagram link on Instagram, and a web page on that website. Those websites see a normal request from your phone (including your IP address), under their own privacy policies. Link Notes sends nothing else to them.

Microphone, voice typing and voice notes

Link Notes asks for the microphone only when you tap the mic or record a voice note.

  • Voice typing and writing out voice notes use your phone's own speech service (on most phones, Google's speech service). When the language is downloaded on your phone, this happens on the phone itself. Otherwise your phone's speech service may process the audio online, under that provider's privacy policy (for Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy). Link Notes does not send audio anywhere itself.
  • Voice notes are saved as audio files on your phone only.

Backups

  • Export backup creates one file with your notes, tags, voice notes and pictures, saved wherever you choose (for example Google Drive or your Downloads). You control that file.
  • If Android's automatic backup is on for your Google account, Android may back up the app's notes and settings to your Google account, as it does for most apps. This is managed by Android and Google, not by Link Notes.

Sharing

Link Notes shares nothing by itself. When you tap Share link, Android's share menu sends the link only to the app you choose.

Children

Link Notes is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete any saved item in the app, or uninstall Link Notes to remove everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
